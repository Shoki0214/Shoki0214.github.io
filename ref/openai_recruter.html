そこで、CVEの情報とサーバーのソフトウェア一覧を生成AI（watsonx.ai）に読ませ、影響の有無を判断根拠付きで自動判定して、毎月レポートを配信する仕組みを作りました。最初は最新のLLMでも正解率がほぼ0%でした。私は精度向上を担当し、公式情報を取得して渡す仕組み（RAG）、段階的に考えさせるプロンプト設計、モデルの比較検証を重ねて、実案件で97%まで引き上げました。結果として、1件30分かかっていた調査が6分になり、年間約200時間の削減につながりました。この取り組みは日本IBMの全社大会でExcellence Awardを受賞しています。

My main hands-on experience is a project where I used generative AI to automate vulnerability impact assessment.

IT operations teams have to check whether new CVEs affect their systems. But CVE descriptions are not standardized, so the work is slow and depends on individual judgment.

I built a tool that feeds CVE records and our server software inventory into an LLM on watsonx.ai. It decides whether each system is affected, explains its reasoning, and sends a monthly report. At first, even top models got almost every answer wrong. I was responsible for accuracy. By retrieving the official CVE data, designing step-by-step prompts, and comparing models, I raised accuracy to 97% on real project data. It cut the time per CVE from 30 minutes to 6, saving about 200 hours a year. The project won an Excellence Award at IBM Japan's company-wide event.

For a customer, Mizuho Bank, I used an AI coding assistant to build a Python tool that converts Oracle code for Db2.

I have not yet taken a generative AI solution into production for a customer. But AI accuracy depends heavily on data quality and integration design, and that has been my core work for ten years. Q6

顧客の本当の課題を見抜いた経験は？Tell me about a time you found the customer's real problem.

Honda様のクラウド移行PoCです。当初は、IBM iからSnowflakeへの連携をCDCで実現するという要件でした。しかし、CDCが1件ずつ更新を発行する方式はSnowflakeに合わず、性能は約200TPSにとどまりました。

顧客の実際の運用を改めてヒアリングしたところ、本当に必要なのはリアルタイムではなく、「夜間に前日分の変更を一括で反映すること」だと分かりました。そこで、CDCで変更を取得し、DataStageでS3に書き出し、COPYとMERGEで一括反映する構成を提案・検証しました。結果は1万TPS超、約50倍です。

競合製品はSnowflakeへの反映方式を備えていましたが、IBM i側の変更取得に課題がありました。当社の強みと組み合わせる形で差別化しました。

AIのユースケースでも、顧客が最初に言う要件と本当の業務課題が違うことは多いはずです。そこを捉え直すことが、このロールのディスカバリーだと考えています。

In a cloud migration PoC for Honda, the request was to replicate data from IBM i to Snowflake using CDC. But CDC sent changes one row at a time, which does not suit Snowflake. We only reached about 200 TPS.

I went back and asked how they actually operated. They did not need real time. They needed to apply the previous day's changes in one batch at night. So I changed the design: CDC captures the changes, DataStage writes them to S3, and Snowflake applies them in bulk with COPY and MERGE. We reached over 10,000 TPS, about 50 times faster.

A competitor could load into Snowflake, but struggled to capture changes from IBM i. Combining both strengths was our differentiator.

I think AI use cases are similar. What a customer first asks for is often different from the real business problem. Finding that gap is what discovery means in this role.

At MUFG, we were launching a service for mass retail customers. Under high load, a batch job took two and a half hours, and the go-live was at risk. I found the bottleneck with nmon and db2advis, redesigned the indexes and SQL, and cut it to 40 minutes. The service went live successfully.

At Toyota Finance, mainframe data contained character encodings that did not match the database definition. I built a custom extension module to convert them, and pushed the product team for a permanent fix.

Q11

金融機関でのAI導入の課題は何だと思いますか？What are the challenges of AI adoption in financial services?

3つあると考えています。

  1. データの所在。重要なデータがメインフレームや基幹DBにあり、AIから安全に、鮮度の高い状態で使える形になっていません。
  2. ガバナンスとセキュリティ。個人情報の扱い、監査対応、社内規程との整合が必要になります。
  3. 効果の証明。PoCで「便利そう」で終わり、定量的な効果を示せないまま本番化の判断ができないケースです。

1つ目はCDCやMDMで私が解いてきた領域です。3つ目は、社内のAI活用で精度（0%→97%）と工数削減（年約200時間）を数字で示した経験が活きると考えています。

I see three.

  1. Where the data lives. Critical data sits in mainframes and core databases. It is not ready for AI to use safely and with fresh data.
  2. Governance and security: personal data handling, audit requirements, and internal policies.
  3. Proving value. Many PoCs end with "this looks useful," but without numbers, nobody can decide to go to production.

The first one is what I have solved with CDC and MDM. For the third, my internal AI project will help: I proved its value with numbers, raising accuracy from 0% to 97% and saving about 200 hours a year.
